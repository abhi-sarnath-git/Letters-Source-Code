# Letters — space-age, personal at heart

Confirmed mark: a **beige envelope with a seal containing the letter S**. The S is intentional, per the owner's direction; do not replace it with L. Burgundy wax and a midnight icon background are provisional colour choices around the confirmed beige envelope. Preserve readability at small app-icon sizes. The included PNG is a vector-drawn first interpretation, not a final approved brand.

## Interface

Use midnight navy backgrounds, ice-cyan actions, restrained violet atmospheric light, and sparse static stars. Keep ordinary conversations readable and familiar. Decorative details never sit behind message text. Use plain names such as Chats, Calls, Updates, Search and Stickers; do not replace these with space-themed jargon.

Keep contrast clear, tap targets generous, essential actions visible, the composer easy to reach, and errors recoverable. Honor Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, reduced motion and reduced transparency. Keep animations purposeful and short. A light appearance and full accessibility/device verification are outstanding work, not completed claims.

## Stickers

Current local SwiftUI source: six symbol-based Orbit stickers plus individual PNG/JPEG imports with explicit size/dimension limits and accessible names. The picker adds a sticker to a local sample conversation. Imports are held in memory only; nothing is uploaded. Static and animated full sticker packs, persistence, favorites, recents, pack management, creation from photos, download/sharing and encrypted delivery are required future work.

All real sticker content must be handled as encrypted media. Do not introduce a third-party sticker service that receives conversation contents or requires unnecessary access to private photo libraries. Downloading a public pack and delivering a sticker in a private conversation are separate privacy boundaries.
